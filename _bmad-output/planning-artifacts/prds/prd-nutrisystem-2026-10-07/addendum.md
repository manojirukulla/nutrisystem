## Proposed safeguards needing agreement

Public contact leakage through pictures, videos, PDF/image menus and metadata is a cross-feature conflict requiring D-8. Provider ownership verification does not certify meals or health claims. Review calculations must follow visible-content moderation policy. Separate publish/send actions and preservation of administrator-edited originals remain proposals.

## Confirmed operating policies

Initial publishing and Listing edits require SuperAdmin approval; subsequent Menu edits do not. Exact classification of mixed Listing/Menu/Package/media changes remains an implementation decision, rather than an exemption for every upload. SuperAdmin owns ownership verification and claim decisions, with the evidence procedure assigned to that role.

Self-review is prohibited for owners/managers of their own Company. Removal and appeal requests go to SuperAdmin through the app or by email with a Review ID. SuperAdmin decides the request; submission alone does not remove or reinstate a Review. Email intake does not select an automated mailbox integration.

Pictures and videos are supported uploads, extending the existing photographic and supplementary PDF/image-menu scope. Exact formats, limits, processing, accessible alternatives and contact-bearing media handling remain implementation choices. These owner decisions narrow D-8–D-11 without treating every remaining operational detail as approved.

## Pilot translation decision and verification

FR-26 distinguishes browser/platform translation from three app-authored language versions. Provider content remains as entered. Cross-language query matching and app-managed localization/content copies are deferred. English source/mock copy is proposed in the review edition, not recorded as separately human-approved.

Verified on 2026-10-07 using [Chrome Help](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/173424?hl=en) and [Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/use-microsoft-translator-in-microsoft-edge-browser): browsers provide page translation with settings/user language choice. Page-display translation is not evidence of backend search-query translation. Embedded photo/PDF translation and translation of dynamic form/auth states must not be guaranteed by the product requirements; test the intended browser experience during implementation. No browser or translation service SDK is selected by this document.
