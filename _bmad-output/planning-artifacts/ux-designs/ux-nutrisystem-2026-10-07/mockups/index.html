Product brief

The PRD owns scope; UX spines govern these visual proposals. No technology/hosting is selected.

Decisions for review

Menu edits need no approval after publication; listing edits need SuperAdmin approval. SuperAdmin handles ownership and review removal/appeal requests with a review ID. Self-review is prohibited. Pictures and videos are supported. Exact fields, media limits and visual details remain proposals. Budget will be assessed after architecture settles the stack and cloud provider.

GTM is deferred. This review does not publish the site or send invitations.
